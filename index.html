About

In a previous life, I earned an M.Eng. in Information Technology and a B.Eng. in Computer Science from International University, Vietnam National University HCMC, graduating as valedictorian and top of my class. During my M.Eng. and B.Eng., I was mentored by Dr. Tuan-Anh Vu.

I later worked in AI research and engineering at FPT AI Center, where I was mentored by Prof. Ngan Le, Prof. Tung Kieu, and Prof. Anh Nguyen. My research background spans embodied AI, autonomous driving perception, adversarial robustness, video understanding, and trustworthy computer vision.
